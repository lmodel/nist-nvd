# Workflows

* [Build and test (main.yaml)](main.md) - The Build and test workflow, `.github/workflows/main.yaml`: on a push to `main` and every pull request, its `test` job lints, tests and checks the adapt copy across Python 3.10 to 3.14, and its `nvd-feed` job validates NVD's live modified feed.
* [Lint and docs (lint-and-docs.yaml)](lint-and-docs.md) - The Lint and docs workflow, `.github/workflows/lint-and-docs.yaml`: ShellCheck, actionlint, the word budgets of CONTRIBUTING.md and SECURITY.md, markdownlint, lychee and codespell, on a push to `main`, every pull request and Mondays at 06:00 UTC.
* [Upstream watch (upstream-watch.yaml)](upstream-watch.md) - The Upstream watch workflow, `.github/workflows/upstream-watch.yaml`: every Monday at 07:00 UTC and by hand, its `nvd-schemas` job runs `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` against NIST's files, and on a finding, or a check that could not run, opens or updates an issue.
* [Semantic release (semantic-release.yml)](semantic-release.md) - The Semantic release workflow, `.github/workflows/semantic-release.yml`: `plan` previews a pull request's release, and `release`, on a push to `main` in the `release` Environment, promotes the changelog, tags, publishes and dispatches the knowledge release.
* [Knowledge Release (knowledge-release.yaml)](knowledge-release.md) - The Knowledge Release workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-release.yaml`: on a published release once `KNOWLEDGE_RELEASE_ENABLED` is `true`, or when dispatched, it attaches the schema copy, the bundle zip and the Copilot skill zip, each with its `.sha256` and an attestation.
* [Knowledge Registrar (knowledge-registrar.yaml)](knowledge-registrar.md) - The Knowledge Registrar workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-registrar.yaml`: the gate of the sidecar. It validates the bundle and its conventions, and checks that each new `human:` confirmation is backed by an approval or a signed commit.
* [Knowledge Librarian (knowledge-librarian.yaml)](knowledge-librarian.md) - The Knowledge Librarian workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-librarian.yaml`: every Monday at 05:00 UTC, once `KNOWLEDGE_LIBRARIAN_ENABLED` is `true`, it runs the ktl-librarian skill with no write token and opens a pull request a person reviews.
* [Knowledge sync (knowledge-sync.yaml)](knowledge-sync.md) - The Knowledge sync workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-sync.yaml`: every Monday at 04:10 UTC it copies the sidecar files from KTL's newest release when the skills pin is behind, and opens one pull request.
* [Knowledge website (knowledge-website.yaml)](knowledge-website.md) - The Knowledge website workflow, `.github/workflows/knowledge-website.yaml`: it builds the site on a change to its inputs and on Mondays at 05:23 UTC, and its `deploy` job publishes to GitHub Pages from `main` once `KNOWLEDGE_WEBSITE_ENABLED` is `true`.
